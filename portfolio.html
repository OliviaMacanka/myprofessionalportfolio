360 Exchange

Date: Current Project | Assistantship

Introduction: The 360 VR Exchange is an Edtech tool that I am working on in collaboration with Dr. Cherner, program director of MEITE. To integrate VR into classrooms, we have been developing a website where teachers can upload, manipulate, and create lessons with 360 videos that we upload. All videos are uploaded to YouTube and to the Pexels account, where we host the 360 content. Conclusion: Working on this project has given me insight into accessibility, UX/ UI design principles, and how to collect and incorporate user feedback effectively. Dr. Cherner and I have been developing a partnership with Fayetteville Street Elementary School to continue development for the classroom.

Howard University Website Redesign

Date: Current Project | Internship

Introduction: While in my internship placement with Modern Campus, I have been assigned to work on redesigning the Howard Community College website. Our goal is to transfer content from their original layout to Modern Campus’ template, while also making the content accessible and easier to navigate. I utilize local components, snippets, assets, and the native CMS system to fix broken links, embed new source code where necessary, and create new layouts.
