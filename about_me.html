Professional Background

I began my career by completing my undergraduate degree at Stockton University with a BA in Literature and a K-12 Teaching License. After my student teaching placements, I found there was a lack of adaptive technology in the classroom, which made the teaching profession more arduous and disatifying for faculty and staff. I was then accepted into UNC Chapel Hill Master of Arts in Educational Innovation, Technology, and Entrepreneurship program, where I am currently learning the technical skills required to develop intentional and accessible tools to implement in secondary school classrooms.

Career Aspirations & Goals

My goal while working in the MEITE program is to create software that meets the demands of the educational market. I am currently researching financial literacy curriculum and how to encourage students to interact meaningfully with the content on a digital interface. Throughout my career I will continue to promote and develop educational technology that is student-centered and accessible for both students and teachers. I screen every feature against teacher feedback before inital product release.

Interests

When I’m not designing learning materials, I am learning more about UX/UI design. I am currently taking a course on Figma, a popular design tool for product developers. On campus, I am currently working with the Student Technology Council, where students work with the UNC IT Department to create campus initiatives regarding inclusive technology and solution-oriented action. I am also excited to connect with the UX/UI design club to learn more about building digital products!
